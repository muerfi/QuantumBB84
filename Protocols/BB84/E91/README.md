# E91 Protocol

## Principle
E91, proposed by Ekert in 1991, uses entangled pairs to generate a key. Unlike BB84, the security relies on the violation of Bell's inequalities.

## Physics
- **Initial state**: \( |\Phi^+\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|00\rangle + |11\rangle) \).
- **Measurements**: Alice and Bob measure in random bases (Z, X, 45°).
- **Bell's inequality**: \( S = 2\sqrt{2} \) in the absence of Eve.

# Comparison of Protocols

## BB84 vs E91
- **BB84**: Based on superposition and no-cloning. Simple but sensitive to imperfections in the sources.
- **E91**: Based on entanglement. More theoretically robust but requires entanglement generators.
